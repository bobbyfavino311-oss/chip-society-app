# REWARD RULES
Each reward may be claimed only once during its eligible streak day.
The player must claim the current reward before advancing to the next day.
After claiming Day 7:
• Mark the seven-day cycle complete.
• Restart the streak at Day 1 for the next eligible daily claim.
• Do not award Day 7 more than once in the same cycle.
• Do not accidentally reset the player before the Day 7 reward is collected.
Use server time for all streak calculations.
Do not use the device clock as the source of truth.

# STREAK CONTINUITY
A valid new streak day begins after the configured daily reset.
The system must prevent:
• Multiple claims in one reset period
• Changing the device clock to claim early
• Duplicate claims after restarting the app
• Duplicate claims from multiple devices
• Skipping directly to a later reward
Save the following server-side:
• Current streak day
• Last successful claim timestamp
• Current streak-cycle identifier
• Whether today's reward was claimed
